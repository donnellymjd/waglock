## Video thumbnail fix — D2S-126

[Video-thumbnail proportions](https://linear.app/d2sci/issue/D2S-126) now use a shared `VideoThumbnailImage` in the Photos grid, Videos cards and album rows. Images scale to fit their intrinsic ratio within the surrounding tile, with black letterboxing. Portrait, landscape, square and orientation-tagged previews are no longer stretched into the tile's ratio. Photo thumbnail cropping is unchanged. Existing video thumbnail/original bytes are not rewritten. Original generation already applies the video track transform; this change fixes presentation.

`VideoThumbnailPresentationTests` renders the actual SwiftUI component and checks the visible pixel bounds, centering and proportions for portrait, landscape, square and rotated images. A square or 16:9 tile can still contain padding around a portrait video; this is intentional preservation of the video frame, rather than distortion or cropping.
