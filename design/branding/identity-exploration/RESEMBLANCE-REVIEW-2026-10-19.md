# Vaultito circular V logos — preliminary IP and resemblance review

Reviewed October 19, 2026. Scope: Open Circle (six Vs), Prism (eight Vs), and the related Orbit treatment (twelve Vs). The owner's phrase “open prism” was treated as covering both Open Circle and Prism.

## Finding and design recommendation

**Do not treat this family as cleared for release. Revise the symbol before publishing it as Vaultito's identity, or obtain a qualified trademark review of the specific proposed mark and intended markets.**

A close pre-existing visual construction was found: **Vivid Photo Visual** uses multicolored inward-facing chevrons arranged around an open circular center. This is materially closer to our proposals than the general rainbow/petal association with Apple Photos. **ConvergeHub**, a software business with an iOS app, also uses a gradient circle containing inward arrows. These findings change the earlier recommendation: Open Circle's small-size advantage is still a design observation, but it is not a recommendation to release the current geometry.

This is a preliminary visual screen and limited record lookup, not a legal opinion or a comprehensive clearance search. The comparisons below are design judgments. They do not establish infringement, ownership, registration, priority, enforceability or likelihood of confusion in a particular jurisdiction.

## What the searches did and did not establish

Web and image queries included:

- `logo eight V shapes circle rainbow`
- `logo rainbow circle v chevrons`
- `logo inward arrows circle colorful`

These were text-driven image searches followed by direct inspection of the strongest candidates. They were **not** reverse-image searches using the proposed Vaultito file, and no unpublished logo file was uploaded to an external service.

The official [USPTO trademark search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) was accessible in the browser. With the Wordmark search and both live/dead statuses enabled:

- `ConvergeHub` returned **No results found**. This narrow result does not establish that the symbol is unregistered or unprotected; different wording, ownership or a design-only record could be relevant.
- The input `"Vivid Photo Visual"` returned **6,517 broad results**, including unrelated VIVID and PHOTO marks. The simple interface did not constrain this to the exact phrase as expected. This result is **inconclusive**, not an absence finding; no matching Vivid logo registration was verified.
- `Vaultito` returned **No results found**. This is only an exact-spelling wordmark check, not name clearance: phonetic variants, similar names, unregistered use and non-US rights remain unassessed.

The [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/) could not be opened in the browser because access was blocked by the browser/client. No WIPO result set or image-similarity search was obtained. WIPO's [database guidance](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) also recommends considering national/regional registers in addition to its collections.

No comprehensive USPTO design-code search, registry image-similarity search, UKIPO/EUIPO/national-register search, copyright-registration search or design-patent search was completed. No registration numbers, owners of specific IP rights, legal status or first-use dates for these designs have been verified. Page timestamps, footer years and the presence/absence of a trademark symbol were not treated as proof of priority or protection.

## Copyright and trademark are different questions

The drawings were generated procedurally from our own geometry; no Vivid or ConvergeHub asset was traced or used to construct them. That design history does **not** establish freedom to use a similar brand symbol.

In the US, trademark concerns include similarity of the marks and the relationship between the goods/services, rather than only whether artwork was copied. See [USPTO likelihood of confusion](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion). A missing federal registration does not resolve the question: US trademark rights can arise from use without federal registration. See [USPTO registration and common-law rights](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/why-register-your-trademark).

Copyright concerns the protectable expression in a drawing, rather than ownership of the general idea “rainbow Vs in a circle.” The [US Copyright Office's Circular 33](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf) explains the distinction between unprotectable familiar symbols/common patterns and potentially protectable original combinations. This review does not determine whether any particular reference or Vaultito drawing meets that threshold. Other countries' rules may differ.

## Practical next step

Retain the owner's preferred warmth, ombré spectrum and V idea, but change the **organizing structure**, rather than relying only on recoloring, changing the unit count, thickening strokes or rounding tips. A single distinctive V with a spectrum treatment, an asymmetric open arc or a non-radial composition are possible directions to explore; none has been checked or is represented as cleared here.

For a final selected design, a trademark professional can assess the mark and name in the intended release territories, investigate the specific Vivid and ConvergeHub references, and search relevant software/app and related services records (potentially classes 9 and 42, without assuming class boundaries decide confusion). No registration, filing, purchase, contact with another rights holder or app integration was performed as part of this review.
